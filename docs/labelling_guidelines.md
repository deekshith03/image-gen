# Labelling guidelines

Label **what is actually in the image** against the **brief**: the product photo plus geography, season and required text. Ignore how the image was made. Planted edits sometimes don't happen, and natural images sometimes fail.

**Principle: strict on content, free on presentation.**

## Text
- **Pass:** the required headline appears with exactly the same words and characters.
  - Case, line breaks and spacing don't matter.
  - **Punctuation is strict:** any added, missing or changed punctuation fails, including a trailing full stop. Typographic variants of the *same* mark count as equal (’ = ', “ = ", – = -).
  - Apostrophes, hyphens, `%`, currency symbols and digits **must** match (`0FF` ≠ `OFF`, `$89` ≠ `$99`).
- **Fail:**
  - missing, extra or misspelled words
  - a duplicated headline or duplicated words
  - garbled extra text near the headline
  - a headline too faint or small to read
- Text that belongs to the product's own label is ignored. Scene text such as signs only fails if it is garbled and prominent.
- Font, colour and placement never fail the text check. Those go under *appeal*.

## Product
- **Pass:** it's recognisably *this* product. Shape, colours, logo, label text, proportions and count are intact.
- **Fine:** angle, lighting, reflections, snow or water on it, scale in the scene.
- **Fail:**
  - recolour
  - changed or misspelled logo
  - missing or added parts (e.g. the charm hung on a necklace)
  - a different product
  - the product missing entirely (e.g. only the food pictured on the box)

## Context
Geography means the **target market**, not a postcard backdrop.

| Field | Pass when |
|---|---|
| **Season fit** | Weather, light, vegetation and clothing match the season *in that market*. Watch the hemisphere: December in Sydney is summer. Tropical markets have wet and dry seasons. |
| **Market fit** | It fits and feels right for that market, and **nothing contradicts it**: wrong country's landmark, wrong festival, foreign signage, wrong climate. A subtle, believable scene **passes**. It doesn't need to shout the city name. |
| **No cliché** | No famous landmark forced in as the location signal, no stereotype, no token props placed only to signal nationality (e.g. chai and flip-flops next to a moisturiser). Real places and landmarks are fine when they fit the product or mood naturally. **India: judge fully. Other regions: the expert panel's verdict and reasons are shown — keep it unless you can see it is clearly wrong; mark `unsure` only if you genuinely can't tell.** |

## Visual integrity (flag, not a brief check)
Awkward crops (a head cut off abruptly), distorted anatomy, melted objects, obvious AI artefacts.

## Appeal (optional, 1–5)
Would you run this ad? Covers typography blending (colour and light match, scale), composition and realism. It ranks ads and never fails one.

## Unsure
Use `unsure` whenever you genuinely can't decide. Those items are excluded from scoring and reported separately.
